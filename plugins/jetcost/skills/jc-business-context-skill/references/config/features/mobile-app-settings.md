### SESSION-DURATION
How long the app keeps a session alive without activity before
considering it expired — 30 minutes on every market, and never changed
since. Changing
it changes what an app session *is*, and therefore app session counts;
see [data/sessions-and-users.md](/data/sessions-and-users.md) for what a
session is and when its expiry is actually evaluated on app.
