# Business Overview

> Full reference document. For the condensed agent context see [overview.md](/overview.md).

## What Jetcost Does

Jetcost is a flight metasearch engine: it aggregates flight offers
from hundreds of partners — including OTAs, airlines, and booking
platforms — and presents them to users searching for flights.
Jetcost does not sell flights directly. Its role is to connect
users with the right offer and redirect them to the partner's
platform to complete the booking.

## Revenue Model

Jetcost generates revenue through two main streams:

- **Core revenue**: earned every time a user clicks out to a
  partner. Depending on the deal type, this is either a fixed
  cost-per-click (CPC) or a commission on completed bookings
  (CPA). Core revenue is the primary and most stable stream.

- **Media revenue**: earned through display advertising and
  sponsored placements (MAL). This stream is independent of
  click monetisation and adds a layer of revenue on top of
  core traffic.

## How the Funnel Works

A user session follows a sequential funnel:

1. **Session** — user lands on Jetcost
2. **Search** — user performs a flight search
3. **Impression** — search results are displayed, each showing
   one or more offers per itinerary
4. **Click** — user selects an offer and is redirected to the
   partner platform
5. **Booking** — user completes the purchase on the partner
   platform (relevant for CPA deals)

Each step has a corresponding conversion rate (STR, CTR, CR)
that measures funnel efficiency.

## Partners

Jetcost works with hundreds of partners across different
categories: global OTAs (e.g. Booking.com, Kayak), regional
OTAs, low-cost carriers, and full-service airlines. Each
partner has a commercial deal that defines the monetisation
model (CPC or CPA), rates, and conditions.

## Platforms

Jetcost operates across two contexts:

- **Web**: the primary platform, accessed via browser
- **App**: the mobile application, with distinct
  monetisation characteristics and strategic importance
  for CPA markets

The distinction between web and app is significant both
commercially (different revenue mix) and strategically
(w2a initiatives aim to shift users from web to app).

## Geographic Presence

Jetcost operates across multiple countries and languages.
Markets differ significantly in deal structure, user
behaviour, and revenue potential. See [core/business/markets.md](/core/business/markets.md) for
a detailed breakdown.
