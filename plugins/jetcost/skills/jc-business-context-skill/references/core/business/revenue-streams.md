# Revenue Streams

## Overview

Jetcost generates revenue through five distinct streams,
grouped into two macro-categories: **Core** and **Media**.
Understanding the distinction between streams is critical
for interpreting metrics, evaluating partner deals, and
assessing the impact of product changes.

## Core Revenue

Core revenue is generated through the click monetisation
funnel — it depends directly on users performing searches
and clicking out to partner platforms.

### Organic Core

The primary revenue stream. Generated by standard partner
integrations: when a user clicks on a flight offer,
Jetcost earns either a fixed fee (CPC) or a commission
on completed bookings (CPA), depending on the deal type.

This is the most stable and structurally important stream.
Its health is measured through funnel metrics: STR, CTR,
Mix CPC/CPA, Average CPC, CR, and Payoff.

### MAL Core

MAL (Meta Advertising Layer) placements that compete
directly within the search results funnel. These are
sponsored positions — partners pay to appear prominently
in the results page alongside organic results.

MAL Core is distinct from Organic Core because the
placement is paid separately by the partner, independent
of the standard CPC/CPA deal. It represents additional
monetisation on top of the organic integration. See
[core/commercials/mal/overview.md](/core/commercials/mal/overview.md) for how MAL Core relates to MAL Media as a
single initiative.

## Media Revenue

Media revenue is generated through advertising placements
and is structurally independent of the click monetisation
funnel. It does not require a user to click out to a
partner to generate revenue.

### Display Media (GAM + AFS)

Advertising served through two integrations: Google Ad
Manager (GAM), banners on web and app paid per impression,
and AdSense for Search (AFS), query-matched text ads on web
only paid per click. Both are priced by the advertising
market independently of Jetcost's partner deals.

This stream is sensitive to traffic volume and page
layout changes, but not directly to partner deal quality.
See [core/media/gam.md](/core/media/gam.md) and
[core/media/afs.md](/core/media/afs.md) for each integration.

### MAL Media

Advertising spaces outside the standard search funnel — dedicated
media slots (e.g. checkbox, inline units, exit units). Part of these
clicks is sold **directly**, through the internal `MDFLOW` network, to
partners Jetcost has a commercial relationship with; the rest come
through the three external networks — `clicktripz`, `kayak` and
`viajala` — that supply the demand, much like a Core backfill provider
supplies offer content. The mix between the two moves over time: query
it for the period at hand. See [core/commercials/mal/networks.md](/core/commercials/mal/networks.md)
and [core/commercials/integration-types.md](/core/commercials/integration-types.md)
for the direct-vs-network-sourced distinction.

That split is also what MAL Media's partner identity depends on. MAL
Core carries the same registry identity as that partner's Core clicks;
on MAL Media, `groupCode`/`partnerCode` are present only for the
`MDFLOW` share, while the buyer itself (`networkPartner`) is recorded
on every MAL click from 2026-01-01 — Jetcost included, bidding on its
own white labels (see
[core/commercials/mal/self-trailingtab.md](/core/commercials/mal/self-trailingtab.md)).

**Important**: MAL Media is currently zero on app.
Initiatives that exclusively impact MAL Media have no
effect on the app perimeter. See
[core/commercials/mal/overview.md](/core/commercials/mal/overview.md)
for the full picture of MAL as one commercial tool producing revenue
in two buckets.

## Data Monetisation

An emerging stream that enables Jetcost to sell
anonymised user data to partners. Data is aggregated
and anonymised before any transfer — no personally
identifiable information is shared.

This stream is currently in early stages. Revenue
amounts are not yet significant and data is not yet
stable enough for use in standard performance
evaluations (e.g. ICE framework). It is tracked
separately and excluded from core business metrics
until further notice. See
[core/media/data-monetization.md](/core/media/data-monetization.md) for the privacy
constraints and configuration.

## FPRS

A new stream: a subscription that sells partners insight on where they
ranked in users' searches, whether or not they took the click. It is
paid apart from the partner's CPC/CPA deal and leaves that deal
untouched, so it belongs to neither Core nor Media. See
[core/commercials/fprs.md](/core/commercials/fprs.md).

## Stream Summary

| Stream | Macro | Depends on funnel | Web | App |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic Core | Core | Yes | ✓ | ✓ |
| MAL Core | Core | Yes | ✓ | ✓ |
| Display Media | Media | No | ✓ | ✓ |
| MAL Media | Media | No | ✓ | ✗ |
| Data Monetisation | — | No | ✓ | ✓ |
| FPRS | — | Searches only, not clicks | tbd | tbd |
