# Magic Method

## Overview

"Magic method" is the internal name for a pricing technique used by
some OTAs to take the first position in the results: the partner
returns a **lower price that is valid only with an outdated payment
method that virtually no user will actually pay with**. There is no
established industry term for it; in plain terms it is
payment-method-dependent lead pricing.

## Why It Works

The offer is ranked on the price the partner returns, and the price
gap the technique creates is large. That gap is enough to put the
partner first, ahead of offers priced on payment methods people
actually use.

## What It Costs the Partner

The price the user saw is not the price they end up paying once they
pick a payment method they really use, so these offers **decay more
than ordinary ones along the funnel** — more users drop between the
click and the booking. The partner trades that higher drop-off for
the visibility of the top position, and over the long run the trade
probably pays off for them.

## Typical Signature

The technique usually leaves two marks on the partner's numbers, in
opposite directions:

- **More clicks**: the bait price draws users to the offer, so the
  partner's click volume rises.
- **Lower CR**: users click, find the price does not hold for the
  payment method they actually use, and do not book, so the partner's
  [CR](/data/metrics.md) falls.

When reading a partner whose clicks grow while its CR drops, the magic
method is one possible explanation worth checking.
