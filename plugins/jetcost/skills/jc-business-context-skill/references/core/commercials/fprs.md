# FPRS — Flight Price Ranking Service

## Overview

FPRS sells partners **insight built on the searches users run on
Jetcost**. For every search, a partner that subscribes is told where it
ranked, **even when it took no click**. The point is to show the
partner how far behind it was and what it could optimise on its own
side to be ranked better.

It is sold as a **subscription**: only partners that join it receive
the insight.

## Why It Is a Revenue Stream of Its Own

The FPRS deal is **paid separately** from the partner's commercial deal
and **does not touch the core deals**: a partner's CPC or CPA terms
(see [core/business/deal-types.md](/core/business/deal-types.md)) stay
what they are whether it subscribes or not. So FPRS revenue sits next
to the existing streams, as a dedicated one, rather than inside Core or
Media (see
[core/business/revenue-streams.md](/core/business/revenue-streams.md)).

Two things follow when reading it:

- **It is earned on searches, not on clicks.** What is sold is the
  ranking a search produced, so FPRS revenue does not move with CTR,
  CR or the mix of CPC and CPA the way Core revenue does.
- **It is not part of a partner's core performance.** A partner's
  FPRS subscription must not be added to its Core revenue, or read as
  a better deal, when evaluating the partner.

It is one of the new streams of the revenue diversification
initiative, INIT-947 in
[core/business/initiatives.md](/core/business/initiatives.md).
