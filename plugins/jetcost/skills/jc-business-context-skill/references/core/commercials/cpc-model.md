# CPC Model

## Overview

The CPC model has a single deal structure: the partner pays a
fixed fee for each click generated by Jetcost, regardless of
whether the user completes a booking. There are no sub-types
or variants — unlike CPA, CPC is always a flat per-click fee.

See [core/business/deal-types.md](/core/business/deal-types.md) for the conceptual explanation of CPC vs CPA
and the revenue formula.

## Key Properties

- One deal type only: fixed fee per click
- Revenue is recognised at click time, not at booking time
- No conversion tracking required — Jetcost has no dependency
  on partner booking data for revenue calculation
- CPC rate varies by partner, market, and route type, but the
  structure of the deal does not
