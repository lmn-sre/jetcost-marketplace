# Product Overview

## Overview

Describes Jetcost's product surface: the search
experience, result page, and key features that
affect funnel conversion. Product decisions
directly impact STR, CTR, CR, and revenue mix.

## Files

- [abtests.md](/core/product/abtests.md) - what an A/B test is, how its perimeter is defined, and how exposed sessions are segmented in the data
- [detail-page.md](/core/product/detail-page.md) - the detail page feature that shifts conversion from blind clicking to informed selection, and how its clickout-mode configuration affects comparability of commercial metrics
- [features.md](/core/product/features.md) - what a 'feature' (operational config lever) is and how its configuration structure works
- [flights.md](/core/product/flights.md) - the flight domain terminology — itinerary, trip, segment, offer — and flight/search type distinctions
- [traffic-control.md](/core/product/traffic-control.md) - what a traffic control (holdout) is, how it differs from an A/B test, how controlled sessions are recorded, and what each code is for
- [travel-stories.md](/core/product/travel-stories.md) - the app's story-format discovery surface, its editorial/Deal Scout split, and how its impression and CTA-click numbers should be read
- [seo-pages.md](/core/product/seo-pages.md) - the static destination/origin/route pages shared by organic and SEM landing traffic, and the legal 15-day limit on their cached prices
- [sorting.md](/core/product/sorting.md) - how flight offer sorting works and why it's one of the highest-leverage product decisions
- [web-vs-app.md](/core/product/web-vs-app.md) - behavioural, funnel, and revenue differences between the web and app platforms
- [metrics.md](/core/product/metrics.md) - product's own app-side metrics: First Opens, the app revenue components, ARPU per install cohort, and return sessions as a retention proxy
- [levers.md](/core/product/levers.md) - A/B tests enabled/disabled/rescoped via admin3's abtest tool, and the `internal_event_type` values that identify them on operation logs
- [w2a/](w2a/index.md) - strategies and tools aimed at migrating users from web to app
