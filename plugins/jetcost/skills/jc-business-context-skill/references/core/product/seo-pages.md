# SEO Pages

## Overview

SEO pages are the static pages built around a destination, an origin
or a route. They are named after their original purpose — ranking on
organic search — but they are not an organic-only surface: they are
also the landing page of several SEM campaign types (see the landing
page table in [core/marketing/sem/index.md](/core/marketing/sem/index.md)).
Anything true of the page therefore applies to both channels; see
[core/marketing/organic.md](/core/marketing/organic.md) for the organic
side.

## Cached Prices and the 15-Day Limit

The prices shown on an SEO page are **cached by nature**: the page is
not the result of a live search, so the fares it displays come from
earlier searches, not from partners at the moment the user looks at it.

For **legal reasons**, a price shown on an SEO page **must never be
older than 15 days**. The limit exists so the page does not advertise
a fare that is no longer up to date.

The limit binds the page, whichever channel brought the user there —
an SEM landing inherits it exactly as an organic visit does.
