# Paid Remarketing

## Overview

Paid campaigns aimed at users who have **already been on Jetcost** and
left a signal of intent, to bring them back. They are not acquisition
of new users: the audience exists only because Jetcost's own traffic
created it, so remarketing grows with the traffic the other channels
bring and cannot replace them.

## Criteo

Running today, on the **web site only**. Jetcost sends signals to
Criteo through GTM — the Criteo tag sends flight searches, see
[core/martech/tags.md](/core/martech/tags.md) — and Criteo uses them to
target, on its display network, users who have already searched on
Jetcost. The tag fires only with the user's consent, so the audience
shrinks and grows with the consent rate, independently of how the
campaigns are run.

Criteo campaigns are switched on and off by hand and declared in the
operation logs by the marketing team (see
[levers.md](/core/marketing/levers.md)). Which channel group and cost
bucket its traffic lands in is in
[other-channels.md](/core/marketing/other-channels.md).

## Paid social (Instagram / Facebook)

Remarketing on Meta's platforms, due to start **by the end of 2026**.
Not to be confused with the organic posts on the same platforms, which
are brand activity (see [social.md](/core/marketing/social.md)).

*To be documented when it starts: audiences, markets and surfaces.*
