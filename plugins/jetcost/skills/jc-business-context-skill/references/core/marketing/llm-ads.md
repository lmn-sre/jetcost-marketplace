# Ads in ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## Overview

Paid campaigns bought on OpenAI, whose ads appear **inside ChatGPT**,
contextualised to the conversation: a user talking about a trip can be
shown a Jetcost ad relevant to it. It is search-like advertising — the
intent comes from what the user is asking — on a new surface.

It is paid acquisition and sits in the `llm` channel group; where its
cost lands is in [other-channels.md](/core/marketing/other-channels.md).

The OpenAI tag sends Jetcost's conversions back to the platform, and
fires only with the user's consent (see
[core/martech/tags.md](/core/martech/tags.md)).

## Not AI referral

Users who reach Jetcost by following a link in an AI assistant's answer
— ChatGPT included — without an ad are **unpaid referral**, with their
own virtual campaigns (see the AI assistant referral section of
[sem/dimensions.md](/core/marketing/sem/dimensions.md)). Same assistant,
different channel: only the ads are this file.
