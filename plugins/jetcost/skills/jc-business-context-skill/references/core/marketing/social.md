# Organic Social

## Overview

Organic posts on Jetcost's **Instagram and Facebook** accounts, run by
the marketing team. The goal is mainly **brand**: keeping Jetcost
visible and recognisable, not driving visits to the site.

So the channel is not judged like an acquisition channel. The sessions
it brings to the site are a by-product and are expected to be small; a
low count is not a sign the channel is failing, and its value does not
show up in the funnel metrics.

Paid campaigns on the same platforms are a different thing: they are
remarketing, see [remarketing.md](/core/marketing/remarketing.md).
